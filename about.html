About HumanEdg Global Academy & Study Abroad

Empowering Students and Professionals to Excel Globally

HumanEdg was founded with a clear vision: to bridge the gap between local potential and global opportunities. As a premier educational academy and study abroad consulting agency located in Kochi, we provide tailored counseling, visa guidance, and language coaching to help you transition smoothly into your next academic or professional milestone.

    Culture & Commitment

    Our commitment to student success doesn't end at the airport

    Student-Centric Coaching

    We prioritize one-on-one sessions over batch counseling. Each student receives individual attention to map their GPA, language scores, and budget to the right campus.

    Visa Scrutiny & Compliance

    We strictly follow country-specific embassy guidelines. Our files undergo multi-tier reviews to ensure high visa success rates and avoid document compliance errors.

    Post-Landing Alumni Network

    We connect newly arriving students with senior student groups in destination countries. We help with accommodation checklists, bank accounts, and local orientation.
